Race Day Plan

Plan your race morning to the minute, and the days before and after. Get it as phone reminders, and print a cue card for the race.

Your plan, your amounts. This page never suggests doses or products. Only schedule what you've tested in training. Not medical advice. Plans are saved on this device only; nothing is uploaded.

Start a plan

Start blank, or from an example with the usual race-morning slots and no amounts filled in.

Race

Check these

    Race morning

    Tap an item to change it. The caffeine total adds up whatever you enter; the limit is yours to set.

    Days before and after

    For things on the days around the race: a daily supplement, travel, a shakeout.

    In-race cue card

    Reminders by race segment, e.g. "During Run 3: gel". Print it, or screenshot it for your lock screen.

    Reminders and backup

    Calendar: on iPhone, open the downloaded file and tap Add All; each item gets an alert. Race-morning times are set in the race's time zone; day items happen at that time wherever you are.

    Plan file: a backup, or a way to move this plan to another device (open it with "Open a plan file").
