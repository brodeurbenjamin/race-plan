Race Day Plan

Plan your race day to the minute, and the days before and after. Save it as an image for your phone, or add it to your calendar as reminders.

Your plan, your amounts. Only schedule what you've tested in training. The page never suggests doses or products; the only number it offers is a basic carb-loading amount, if you ask for it. Not medical advice. Nothing is saved or uploaded: the page starts fresh each visit. To keep your plan, save it as an image, add it to your calendar, or save a plan file.

Race

Times your post-race items from your finish. Uses 1:30 if empty.

Racing in another time zone?

Times show in race-local time, so they stay right after you travel.

What do you want in your plan?

Open a category, then tap a suggestion or write in your own. Suggested times are typical, and meals follow your start time; tap an item to change it. Supplements and caffeine pills have no suggested time: you set it.
